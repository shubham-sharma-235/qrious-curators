Video Editor
Full Time Jaipur +

Job Description

We are looking for a creative Video Editor with at least 1 year of experience in editing reels, social media videos, ad creatives, and brand content. The candidate should understand pacing, music sync, transitions, hooks, captions, and platform-friendly video formats.

Responsibilities

  • Edit reels, short videos, ad videos, and social media content.
  • Add captions, transitions, music, effects, and basic motion graphics.
  • Understand brand guidelines and create videos accordingly.
  • Work closely with the content and social media team.
  • Deliver videos on time with proper formatting for Instagram and other platforms.

Requirements

  • Minimum 1 year of video editing experience.
  • Good knowledge of Premiere Pro, After Effects, CapCut, or similar tools.
  • Strong understanding of reels, hooks, transitions, and trending formats.
  • Basic knowledge of color correction and audio balancing.
  • Should be creative, detail-oriented, and deadline-focused.

Job Description

Graphic Designer
Full Time Jaipur +

Job Description

We are looking for a creative Graphic Designer who can design social media creatives, ad banners, brand posts, stories, and marketing visuals. The candidate should have a good sense of layout, typography, colors, and brand aesthetics.

Responsibilities

  • Create social media posts, stories, reels covers, and ad creatives.
  • Design banners, campaign creatives, and brand marketing visuals.
  • Work according to brand guidelines and content requirements.
  • Collaborate with content writers, marketers, and video editors.

  • Good knowledge of Photoshop, Illustrator, Canva, Figma, or similar tools.
  • Strong understanding of social media design and brand aesthetics.

  • Portfolio with social media or branding work is preferred.
  • Should be creative, fast, and open to feedback.
