02 / Our Work

One brand.
Five digital disciplines.

01
Build

Website Development

A responsive commerce experience organised across collections, all products, customer accounts, cart and checkout. The interface gives product imagery room to breathe while keeping every buying action clear and accessible.

  • Collection-first information architecture
  • Responsive e-commerce interface
  • Account, cart and checkout journeys
02
Discover

SEO

The content structure was planned around high-intent jewellery searches — from hoops and earrings to collections, product care and service information — helping both customers and search engines understand the catalogue more clearly.

  • Search-led category architecture
  • SEO-friendly product and collection taxonomy
  • Metadata and on-page content direction
03
Engage

Social Media Marketing

A modern content direction balancing clean product storytelling with everyday styling. The system supports new drops, hero pieces, jewellery education, gifting moments and community-led communication.

  • Campaign and content planning
  • Product drops and styling storytelling
  • Reels, statics and carousel systems
04
Present

Photography & Visual Direction

Jewellery needs proportion, texture and shine to translate online. The visual direction uses controlled contrast, close crops and minimal styling to make hoops, rings, chains and statement pieces feel contemporary and premium.

  • Product and campaign visual direction
  • Composition and styling guidance
  • Image selection and digital optimisation
05
Grow

Digital Marketing

The marketing layer connects product drops and campaign creative with high-intent journeys. Audience strategy, retargeting and cart-focused communication help move discovery towards purchase.

  • Campaign creative and landing direction
  • Audience and retargeting strategy
  • Product-led conversion communication
03 / Experience Architecture

Built around a cleaner path to purchase.

The experience connects discovery, account access, cart progress and customer-service information into one coherent journey — without making the interface feel crowded.
