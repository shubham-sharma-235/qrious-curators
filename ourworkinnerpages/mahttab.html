About the Project

How we shaped Mahttab’s digital experience

We worked on Mahttab’s digital presence as an agency case study — building the direction, structure, and execution system needed for a premium jewellery brand to communicate clearly online. Our focus was not just on visuals, but on how the website, content, product storytelling, and campaigns worked together.

From page flow and product presentation to social communication and launch assets, every touchpoint was created to make the brand experience more consistent, polished, and conversion-ready.

Services

  • We defined the digital direction for Mahttab by clarifying positioning, audience expectations, tone, and premium brand cues before moving into execution.

  • We translated the brand’s elegance into a clean website interface with soft visuals, clear sections, and a browsing flow suited for jewellery discovery.

  • We structured the Shopify experience so collections, product pages, navigation, and the purchase flow supported smooth online shopping.

  • Every layout, interaction, and section was planned around user intent — discovery, trust, product detail, and purchase clarity.

  • We created direction for product visuals, mood, framing, and presentation so the imagery supported the website and campaign language.

  • We built a social communication system with product-led storytelling, festive content angles, campaign hooks, and a consistent visual language.
