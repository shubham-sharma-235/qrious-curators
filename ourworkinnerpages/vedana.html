What We Did

From website experience to content systems, we built the digital direction

Our role was to make Vedana’s digital presence clearer, more premium, and easier to consume. We structured the brand communication so shoppers could understand collections, offers, reasons to buy, and campaign stories faster across web and social touchpoints.

The case study keeps Vedana’s soft visual identity intact, but the storytelling speaks from Qrious Curators’ side — what we created, how we planned it, and how the work supported the brand’s online growth.

Agency Scope

  • We refined the website experience direction so the brand could feel premium, easy to browse, and aligned with Vedana’s calm fashion identity.

  • We organized the communication around clear shopping intent — category discovery, offer visibility, product benefits, and easy-to-scan sections.

  • We created offer-led messaging and campaign sections that make discounts, festive drops, and product highlights more visible without making the brand look cluttered.

  • We shaped the product story around fabric, fit, print, mood, and occasion so the customer understands value before clicking into a product page.

  • We built a social-first direction for reels, stories, carousels, hooks, captions, festive pushes, and everyday product-led communication.
