What we worked on

Premium jewellery
shopping experience

Jewellery Strategy

We structured the shopping journey around gold, diamond, and silver jewellery categories so customers can easily discover designs for every special occasion.

UI / UX Design

The experience was designed to feel elegant, trustworthy, and premium while keeping jewellery browsing simple across rings, earrings, pendants, necklaces, and bracelets.

E-commerce Flow

We planned a smooth jewellery shopping flow with product discovery, wishlist actions, cart movement, and checkout steps built for easy purchase decisions.

Collection Navigation

Clear category paths were built around gold jewellery, diamond jewellery, silver utensils, gifting, price ranges, and product types for effortless browsing.

Product Presentation

Jewellery pieces were presented with strong visual focus, refined category naming, and trust-building cues like certified jewellery, hallmarking, and diamond assurance.

Mobile Responsive Experience

The layout was planned to keep jewellery discovery, product visuals, and shopping actions clean and accessible across desktop, tablet, and mobile screens.
